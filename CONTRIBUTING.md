# Contributions and maintenance

SagePreview is distributed under the MIT License. Forks and experimentation are welcome.

There is **no promise of ongoing maintenance, issue triage, reviews, or pull-request merges**. Maintainer availability may be limited. If you need a change, a fork may be the fastest path.

When sharing improvements, please retain the MIT notice and the separate third-party notices. Test changes on Windows 11 x64 and avoid adding unrelated features, telemetry, subscriptions, or intrusive Explorer modifications to this project's codebase.
